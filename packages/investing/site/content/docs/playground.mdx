Everything on this page imports the package's own source files — `src/predictos/agents/mapper-agent.ts`,
`src/correlate/metrics.ts`, `src/correlate/rolling-stats.ts` and `src/predictos/data/polymarket.ts` — and
runs them client-side. Nothing here needs an API key or a network call, because these are the parts of
`investing` that are pure logic. The network-bound calls (quotes, Polymarket, the AI agents) are covered with
copy-paste examples on their own pages.

## Turn an analysis into an order

`runMapperAgent` is the last step of the [PredictOS pipeline](/docs/predictos): it turns an analysis
(`BUY YES`, `BUY NO` or `NO TRADE`) and a market's raw data into Polymarket order parameters. Try a budget
below $1 or above $100, a closed market, a price so high that the budget buys fewer than 5 shares, or switch
the platform to Kalshi to see each guard throw.

## Rolling mean and volatility

`calculateRollingStats` appends `<field>_rolling_mean_<window>d` and `<field>_rolling_std_<window>d` to every
row. Two details worth knowing: rows before the window fills copy the raw value into both columns rather than
leaving them empty, and both columns are floored to whole numbers (`Math.floor`), so use it on prices, not on
returns or probabilities.
