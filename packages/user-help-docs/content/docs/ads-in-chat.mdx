QwkSearch can show two kinds of sponsored placement in a chat, both from the
`ads-in-chat-answers` package. Neither changes what the assistant says: the
answer is written first, without any knowledge of advertisers, and ads are
chosen afterwards.

## What you see

- **A product carousel under an answer.** It has the advertiser's name, an
  **Ad** label and a ⋯ menu where you can hide the ad, see why it was shown,
  ask about it, or report it.
- **One sponsored follow-up question.** In the *Related* list, a row reading
  "Learn more about …" marked **Sponsored** with the advertiser's name. It is
  never the first suggestion.

An ad appears only when your question matches keywords an advertiser chose.
A question that matches nothing shows no ad, however much anyone bids.

## How an ad is chosen

1. Every active campaign is scored against your question (and, at half
   weight, the answer). A negative keyword rules a campaign out.
2. Campaigns under 30% relevance are dropped.
3. The rest are ranked by relevance × the advertiser's maximum cost per
   click; the winner pays just enough to beat the next ad.
4. One advertiser can't fill both the carousel and the follow-up on the same
   answer, and a campaign that has spent its daily budget is skipped.

## For advertisers

The advertiser panel takes a short description of your business and has a
language model propose the keywords people actually ask about, along with
negative keywords and a follow-up topic. You choose placements and products,
set a bid and a daily budget, and can test any question against the live
auction before launching.

A live demo of both placements, the auction inspector and the panel deploys
to Vercel from `packages/ads-in-chat-answers/demo` — see the package README
for setup.
