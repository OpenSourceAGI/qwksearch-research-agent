Save and open tabs

Choose Save tabs beside Open tabs to create a new collection. Check “and close them” to close the tabs after saving. “and switch to new collection” makes the collection current without reopening those tabs; it requires all unpinned tabs in the window and cannot be combined with closing them.

To add tabs to an existing collection, drag them from Open tabs into it. The browser tabs stay open. Open adds fresh tabs from a collection; Switch makes the collection current in the window. The outgoing-work checkbox in the Switch dialog is unchecked by default. Pinned tabs stay open.

Auto-update follows the current window while On. Paused keeps the saved collection fixed. Resuming updates it from the window's current tabs. Saving and switching guide.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Alt+Q: visual switcher.
  • Alt+Shift+Q: library.
  • Alt+Shift+K: search.
  • Alt+W: close the focused tab or group in the switcher, or selected unpinned tabs in selection mode.

Change bindings in your browser's extension shortcut settings. In search, type @ to select a collection or / to choose an action. Up/Down selects a result and Enter opens it. Protected pages use a separate extension window. Search and shortcut guide.

Group cards also have a × close button. Alt+W is inactive while typing. Closing keeps the switcher available with Undo, using a separate window if you close the page hosting it.

The sidebar search finds saved tabs, notes, groups and collections across all spaces, with matches shown inside their collection cards. Press / to focus search. Clear search to return to your previous view.

Group tabs

Auto-group only groups ungrouped tabs. Existing groups keep their membership when titles or URLs change. Tabs you manually ungroup stay independent as you navigate. Explicit Group & sort and AI organization can regroup existing tabs when “Include already grouped tabs” is enabled; both put independent tabs before groups and sort alphabetically. These actions offer Undo.

Backups and recovery

Download Settings → Export & import → Backup JSON before uninstalling or changing installations. Import that file in the destination and check your collections before removing the original. Keys and preview images are excluded.

Use Undo after a supported change. Timeline and collection Version history provide earlier saved work. Open search and choose /recovery for interrupted operations. Recovery restores saved URLs and groups, not unsaved form or app state. Recovery guide.

Privacy and optional connections

Your core library stays in your browser profile. Settings → Privacy & permissions controls automatic previews, cached images and optional permissions. Opening the visual switcher can capture the current page even when automatic previews are off.

AI and Notion send data directly to services you configure. Saving an AI key and granting provider access can enable automatic collection naming. Notion exports are snapshots, not two-way sync. Provider charges may apply. AI setup · Notion setup.
