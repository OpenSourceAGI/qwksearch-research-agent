What happens to my data?

Uninstalling removes LeoTabs' local library, settings and recovery copies. Downloaded backups, browser bookmarks and exports to Notion remain where you saved them.

If you saved a backup, you can import it into another LeoTabs installation. The developer cannot recover a library that was stored only in your browser.
